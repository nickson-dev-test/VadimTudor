Privacy Policy for Tribunul Vadim Tudor

Last updated: 27 October, 2025

Thank you for choosing Tribunul Vadim Tudor. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected (including by third-party SDKs integrated in the app), how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

The app itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data directly. However, the app displays banner advertisements powered by Google AdMob. As part of serving ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect and transmit the following data types off your device:

Data Type Description Purpose
Advertising ID Your device's resettable advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID) Ad targeting & frequency capping
IP Address Network IP address used to derive approximate location Geo-targeted advertising
Device Information Device model, OS version, screen size, language settings Ad rendering & analytics
Ad Interaction Data Ad impressions, clicks, and engagement events Ad performance measurement
Coarse Location General geographic region (country/city level, not precise GPS) Geo-targeted advertising

This data is collected and processed by Google LLC under their own privacy policy. The app developer does not have access to or control over this data.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

The app does not collect or transmit any of the following:

  • Name, email address, or any account / registration information
  • Precise GPS location
  • Health or financial data
  • Contacts, messages, or photos
  • Microphone or camera data
  • Browsing history outside the app

3. Third-Party Services - Google AdMob

The app integrates Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to display banner advertisements. AdMob may use the data types listed in Section 1 to serve personalised or non-personalised ads depending on user consent and regional regulations (e.g. GDPR consent for EEA/UK users).

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • AdMob Data Safety information: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation (or "Delete advertising ID" on Android 12+)

4. How We Use Information

  1. Advertising: AdMob data is used solely by Google to serve and measure banner ads displayed within the app.
  2. Support & Communication: Only if you contact us at nickforgesolutions@gmail.com, we use your email solely to respond to your enquiry.

Tribunul Vadim Tudor is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from minors. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.
